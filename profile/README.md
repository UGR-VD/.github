# UGRacing Vehicle Dynamics

We’re the Vehicle Dynamics subteam of [UGRacing](https://www.ugracing.co.uk/), the University of Glasgow’s Formula Student team. We study how the car corners, brakes and accelerates, and use simulation and logged track data to inform suspension design and setup decisions.

## What we work on

- **Suspension and setup** — model suspension geometry, load transfer, ride heights and the effects of setup changes.
- **Tyres and vehicle performance** — estimate the grip available under cornering, braking and acceleration at different speeds.
- **Lap simulation and data analysis** — compare predicted speed and acceleration with logged laps, and use the results to assess setup changes and estimate loads around the circuit.

Our MATLAB vehicle model and lap-time simulator builds a speed-dependent acceleration envelope and uses it to predict lap performance. We run it on a logged driving line and compare the predictions with the recorded lap.
